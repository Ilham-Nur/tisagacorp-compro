Our Core Service

ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING & INSTALLATION

  • High, Medium, and Low Voltage Power Distribution Systems.
  • Cable Tray Installation, Power & Control Cabling, and Glanding/Termination.
  • Substation Equipment Installation (Transformers, Switchgears, MCC).
  • Grounding and Lightning Protection Systems.

INSTRUMENTATION & CONTROL SYSTEMS

  • Installation of Field Instruments (Transmitters, Control Valves, Gauges).
  • Tubing Fabrication and Impulse Line Installation (SS, Copper, Carbon Steel).
  • Control System Integration (PLC, SCADA, and DCS Systems).
  • Calibration, Loop Testing, and Pre-Commissioning Support.

MAINTENANCE & CALIBRATION SERVICES

  • Routine and Shutdown Maintenance for Electrical & Plant Instruments.
  • On-site Instrument Calibration with Certified Master Equipment.
  • Troubleshooting, Retrofitting, and Upgrading of Legacy E&I Systems.
